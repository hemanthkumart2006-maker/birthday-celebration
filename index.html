Hey love,

I don't know where to start, because every time I think about you, words feel less and emotions feel infinite. Par phir bhi, aaj mann ki baat likhni thi... sirf tumhare liye ❤️

Tum meri zindagi ka wo hissa ho jo bina noise ke sab kuch bright kar deta hai. Jaise soft si roshni jo andhere room ko bhi warm bana deti hai. Tumhara hona loud nahi hai, par powerful hai. Tumhari ek smile poore din ka stress hata deti hai, aur tumhari ek simple "sab theek hai" wali line mujhe strong bana deti hai.

I love the way you talk, even about random things. Tumhari baatein, tumhari choti-choti complaints, tumhari excitement... tumhari baatein jo sirf mujhe sunani hoti hain — sab kuch special lagta hai. Tum normal cheezon ko bhi beautiful bana deti ho, jaise chai, shaam, sadak, music, silence... sab kuch tumse connect ho jaata hai.
